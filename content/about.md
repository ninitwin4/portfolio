## Background

Ni Ni Tin Win co-founded a consumer app company before she ever wrote production code. Chat Chin was Myanmar's first roadside assistance and car servicing app, and she was the person holding engineering, design, and merchant partnerships together because there was nobody else to do it. That is still roughly how she work. She scaled Chat Chin from zero to 2,000+ active users, launching iOS and Android across Myanmar's three largest metropolitan markets, and drove a 25% engagement lift and 15% satisfaction improvement through analytics and A/B testing.

The path since then looks less linear than it is. Design roles, then robotics data operations at a home robotics startup, and now building LLM systems in Python and FastAPI. What connects them is that she has consistently ended up at the boundary between a technical system and the people who depend on it working. At The Bot Company, AI robotics startup, she was the escalation point for data collection production systems, which meant she spent two years learning what actually breaks in production and how to tell a real failure from a noisy one. That is not a background most people bring to AI engineering, and it turns out to matter a great deal when the output of a model drives a real decision.

Which is the question she recent work keeps circling. Her matching engine started with a problem she had myself, roommate pairing, and became an exercise in reliability: how do you keep an LLM trustworthy when its answer is the product? The answer she landed on was a deterministic core with a hard-capped model layer on top, six architecture decision records, and an eval harness with authored golden pairs before any of it shipped.

She care most about the part of a system nobody demos: the eval harness, the graceful degradation path, the validation that stops a bad output before a user sees it.
She is especially interested in product engineering, AI builder roles at early-stage teams where the same person is expected to define the problem, design the interface, and ship the thing.

## What She Works On Now

Building AI systems, with a focus on making AI reliable - data quality, evaluation, and the human layer that makes models trustworthy. Currently deep in evaluation, agent tooling, and the messy parts of shipping AI that works.
She is a continuous learner — she's in an AI engineering program now and building her own tools to sharpen the technical side.
